Why a vault, not just a hidden folder

A folder can be found. A vault is built so that even if someone picks up your phone, your private moments stay private — encrypted, locked, and yours alone.

What's inside

Everything V Vault does is built around one idea: your media, under your control.

Password or PIN lock
Set a password, a numeric PIN, or unlock with your fingerprint or face — your choice, set once and remembered.
Private folders
Organize hidden photos and videos into folders you name, so your vault stays as tidy as your camera roll.
Your own Google Drive backup
Turn on backup and V Vault uploads an encrypted copy to a folder in your own Drive. Nothing is sent to any V Vault server.
Restore on a new device
Signed in with the same Google account, restore your vault from a previous backup in a few taps.
Offline by default
Without backup turned on, nothing ever leaves your device. V Vault works the same with no internet connection at all.

Questions people ask

Does V Vault need an internet connection?

No. Locking, viewing, and organizing your media all work fully offline. An internet connection is only used if you turn on optional Google Drive backup.

Can V Vault see my photos?

No. Your media and password never leave your device unless you choose to back up, and even then the backup is encrypted before it's uploaded to your own Google Drive.

What happens if I forget my password?

You can reset it by answering the security question you set on first launch. This happens entirely on your device.

Is V Vault free?

Yes, V Vault is free to download and use.
